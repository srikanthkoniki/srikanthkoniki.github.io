Academic & Professional Profile

About Me

I am Dr. Srikanth Koniki, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chaitanya Bharathi Institute of Technology (CBIT), Hyderabad.

My academic and professional interests combine teaching, research, construction management and practical field experience. My research focuses on sustainable construction materials, BIM applications, IoT-based infrastructure monitoring, repair and rehabilitation, and structural engineering.

My professional experience includes academic teaching, research coordination, quantity surveying, construction supervision, project monitoring and third-party quality inspection.
